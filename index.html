02 / Behind the signal

Chung-Yi Wang.

Also known as Pluto.
Director / Visual Artist

One practice.
Many ways
of seeing.

I’m Chung-Yi Wang (Pluto), a director and visual artist working across commercials, narrative film, music videos, documentary and new media.

My practice connects visual storytelling with immersive experiences—from the intimacy of a single frame to the possibilities of VR and multi-screen installations.

Alongside filmmaking, my experience spans creative direction, post-production supervision and teaching film, VR and documentary production.
